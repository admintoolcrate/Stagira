How it works

  1. IName it. A vice to stop or cut down, or a virtue to practise. Counted from today, or from a day in the past.
  2. IILive it. The count ticks over at midnight. Nothing to tick off: good days count on their own.
  3. IIIBegin again. Slipped? Log it in two taps. The count restarts, the run you had stays in your history.

What it does

Everything you need to keep going, and nothing to keep you scrolling.

  • Vices and virtues Stop or cut down on something, or do something good each day — on every day or chosen weekdays.
  • Milestones, years ahead Close together at first, then on the real anniversaries, each with a passage from Aristotle.
  • Calendars and stats A month and a year view for each goal. Are your runs getting longer? The ledger shows you.
  • A weekly review The last seven days against the seven before: clean days, slips, urges resisted, milestones.
  • Urges resisted For vices, one tap logs a moment you felt the urge and didn't. Never counted against you.
  • Home-screen widgets One goal, an overview of your week, or your whole ledger. Solid, see-through or clear.

Private by design

What you're working on is your business.

No account, no analytics, no ads and no server of ours. Your ledger is kept on your phone, and leaves it only if you choose to sync.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, every feature for everyone. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no Google ad or billing libraries in the app. If you'd like to help, you can support it on Ko-fi.

What happens when I slip?

Log it in two taps and begin again. The count restarts, but the run you had is kept in your history and stats, and you get a short passage from Aristotle for the day. You can also record a slip on a past day, or remove one.

What's the difference between a vice and a virtue?

A vice is something you want to stop or cut down on, like drinking. A virtue is something good you do, like a daily run or one good deed; it can be due every day or only on the weekdays you choose.

Do I need an account? What if I change phones?

No account. To move to a new phone, turn on sync to your own Google Drive, keep an automatic backup file, or export and import your ledger as a file.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet: Stagira is for Android (8.0 and later). It's coming to Google Play; until then you can try a test version from GitHub.

Why “Stagira”?

It's named after Aristotle's birthplace. He taught that character isn't something you're born with but something you build, one habit at a time. Stagira keeps count of the days you put in.

One fine day doesn't make a life. Many do.
