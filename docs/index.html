Stagira

The Habit Ledger

A quiet ledger of the days you keep. Name something to stop, or something good to do each day, and Stagira counts the days. Good days count on their own; you only log a slip if one happens.

For Android. Coming soon to Google Play.

Goals

  • Drink less 8 days to 50 days 42days
  • One good deed a day Milestone today: 2 weeks 14days
  • Less phone 2 days to 5 days 3days

What it does

  • Tackle a vice or practise a virtue. Stop or cut down on something, or do something good each day.
  • Slips without shame. Log one in two taps and begin again; the run you had stays in your history.
  • Milestones, years ahead. Close together at first, then on the real anniversaries, each with a passage from Aristotle.
  • Calendars, stats and a weekly review. Are your runs getting longer? The ledger shows you.
  • Widgets. One goal, an overview, or your whole ledger on the home screen.

Private by design

  • No account, no analytics, no ads. Your ledger is kept on your phone.
  • Optional sync. To your own Google Drive, encrypted with a passphrase if you like.
  • App lock and private titles. Fingerprint lock, and cover names for goals you'd rather keep to yourself.
  • Free, all of it. Nothing to buy, nothing to unlock.
