How it works

  1. IName it. Something to give up or cut down on, or something good to do. Start from today, or from a day in the past.
  2. IILive it. The count goes up at midnight. No daily check-in needed: every good day counts on its own.
  3. IIIBegin again. Slipped? Log it in two taps. The count starts again, and your previous run stays in your history.

What it does

Everything you need to keep going, and nothing to keep you scrolling.

  • Vices and virtues Give something up, cut down, or build a good habit, every day or just on the weekdays you choose.
  • Milestones, years ahead Frequent at first, then on the anniversaries that matter, each marked with a passage from Aristotle.
  • Calendars and stats Month and year views for every goal, so you can see at a glance whether your runs are getting longer.
  • A weekly review This week compared with last: clean days, slips, urges resisted and milestones.
  • Urges resisted Felt the urge and didn't give in? Log it with one tap. It never counts against you.
  • Home-screen widgets Show one goal, your week at a glance, or your whole ledger, in a solid, see-through or clear style.

Private by design

What you're working on is your business.

No account, no analytics, no ads and no servers of our own. Your ledger stays on your phone unless you choose to sync it.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, every feature, for everyone. There are no ads or in-app purchases, and the app doesn't even contain ad or payment code. If you'd like to help, you can support it on Ko-fi.

What happens when I slip?

Log it in two taps and begin again. The count starts over, but your previous run stays in your history and stats, and you get a short passage from Aristotle for the day. You can also add or remove a slip on a past day.

What's the difference between a vice and a virtue?

A vice is something you want to give up or cut down on, like drinking. A virtue is something good you want to do, like a daily run or one good deed, either every day or only on the weekdays you choose.

Do I need an account? What if I change phones?

No account needed. To move to a new phone, sync with your own Google Drive, keep an automatic backup file, or export your ledger and import it on the new phone.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Stagira is Android-only for now (Android 8.0 and later). It's coming to Google Play soon; in the meantime you can try a test version from GitHub.

Why “Stagira”?

It's named after Aristotle's birthplace. He taught that character isn't something you're born with but something you build, one habit at a time. Stagira keeps count of the days you put in.

One fine day doesn't make a life. Many do.
