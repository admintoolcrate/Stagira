Support us

Keep the ledger free.

No ads, no in-app purchases, nothing to unlock.

Every feature in Stagira is free. It works entirely on your phone and never asks you to sign up. There are no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and we'd like to keep it that way.

Building it, testing it on each new version of Android and answering bug reports all take time. If Stagira has helped you keep a few more days, a donation helps pay for that time. It's entirely optional, and every feature stays free either way.

Other ways to help: report a bug or suggest a feature, or tell a friend who's counting days too.
