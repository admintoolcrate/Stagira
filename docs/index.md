# Stagira

A quiet ledger of the days you keep. Name something to stop or cut down on, or something good
to do each day, and Stagira counts the days. Named after Aristotle's birthplace: he taught that
character is built by habit, one day at a time.

- No account, no analytics, no servers of ours. Your ledger stays on your phone unless you turn
  on sync to your own Google Drive, encrypted if you add a passphrase.
- Free to use, with an optional one-time Plus.
