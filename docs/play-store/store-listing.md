## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Stagira is a quiet record of the days you keep.

Tackle a vice, something to stop or cut down on, like drinking less. Or practise a virtue, something good to do each day, like one good deed. Good days count on their own: there's nothing to log unless something goes wrong. If you slip, log it in a couple of taps and begin again. The run you had stays in your history.

Aristotle, born in Stagira, taught that character isn't something we're born with but something we build, one habit at a time. Stagira keeps count of the days you put in.

WHAT IT DOES
• Day counts for any number of goals, started today or on a day in the past
• Log a slip for today, yesterday or any earlier day, with an optional note
• Hold + for Quick log: any goal's slip, or an urge you resisted, without opening it
• Milestones from your first day to years ahead, falling on the real anniversary
• Progress measured by the gaps between slips: are your runs getting longer?
• Calendars, stats, run charts and a weekly review
• Virtues on the weekdays you choose; rest days never count against you
• Home-screen widgets: one goal, an overview, or several goals at once
• Automatic backup to a file you choose, plus export and import
• Daily or weekly reminders, and a milestone note on the day you reach one
• Optional passages from Aristotle's Nicomachean Ethics, quoted word for word and sourced

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no analytics, no servers of ours, and no ads unless you choose to watch one
• Your ledger is kept on your phone
• Optional sync to your own Google Drive, end-to-end encrypted with a passphrase if you like
• App lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock
• Private titles: show a cover name, and keep the real one behind a PIN

FREE, WITH A ONE-TIME PLUS
Counting, logging, history, the app lock, private titles, backup and two widgets are free. Stagira Plus, one payment and no subscription, adds unlimited goals (free holds 3), Google Drive sync, the multi-goal widget, and insights: the progress card, year view and weekly review. There's also a tip jar, and an optional ad you can watch to support the app.

CALM BY DESIGN
Black and white, light and dark, no badges and no streak-shaming. Turn off the quotes, celebrations or vibration if you'd rather it say less.

Stagira is a personal tracker, not medical advice or treatment. If you're struggling with alcohol, drugs or gambling, please also reach out to a doctor or a local support service.
