## App name (30 characters max)

**Stagirite – Habit Tracker**

## Short description (80 characters max)

Count the days you keep. A private, quiet ledger for habits you're building or breaking.

## Full description (4,000 characters max)

Stagirite is a quiet record of the days you keep.

Name something you want to do less of, a Vice, or more of, a Virtue, and the ledger counts the
days. Slip, and you log it in a couple of taps: the count starts again, and every day you kept
stays in your history.

Aristotle, "the Stagirite", held that character is built by habit, through doing. Stagirite
counts the doing.

WHAT IT DOES
• Day counts for any number of goals, from today or a day in the past
• Vices and Virtues, with Virtues on the weekdays you choose; rest days never count against you
• Log a slip (or a missed day) for today, yesterday or any earlier day, with an optional note
• Log urges you resisted; they're shown as strength, never against you
• Milestones from 1 day to years, with a quiet celebration when you reach one
• Progress measured by the gaps between slips: are your runs getting longer?
• Month and year calendars, stats, run-length charts and a weekly review
• Targets, archive, sorting, a home-screen widget and daily or weekly reminders
• Optional passages from Aristotle's Nicomachean Ethics, quoted word for word and sourced

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no servers of ours
• Your ledger is kept on your phone
• Optional sync to your own Google Drive, end-to-end encrypted with a passphrase if you like
• App lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock
• Private titles: show a cover name, keep the real one behind a PIN
• Reminders and the widget never reveal what your goals are

CALM BY DESIGN
Black and white, light and dark, no badges or streak-shaming. Turn off the quotes,
celebrations or vibration if you'd rather it say less.

Stagirite is a personal tracker, not medical advice or treatment. If you're struggling with
alcohol, drugs or gambling, please also reach out to a doctor or a local support service.
