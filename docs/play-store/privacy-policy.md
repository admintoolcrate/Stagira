# Privacy policy — Stagira

_Effective 25 October 2026_

Stagira is a habit and day-count tracker. This policy explains what happens to the
information you put into it. In short: **it stays on your phone unless you choose to sync it
to your own Google account, and we never receive it.**

## What the app stores

Your goals (names, notes, start dates, slips, urges resisted, schedules, targets and
settings) are stored on your device, in storage only this app can read. There is no Stagira
account and no Stagira server, and no analytics or tracking code. Ads appear only if you
choose to watch one (see below).

## Optional sync with Google Drive

If you turn on sync, the app saves a copy of your ledger to a hidden app-data folder in **your
own** Google Drive, using Google's `drive.appdata` permission. That permission lets the app see
only its own folder, not any of your other files. The copy is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS)
connection. If you set a sync passphrase, the copy is also encrypted on your phone before it
is uploaded (AES-256-GCM), so it can't be read without your passphrase, including by Google or
by us. Google's handling of your Drive is covered by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can stop syncing at any time in Settings → Sync → Disconnect, which also revokes the app's
access and can delete the synced copy.

## Private titles, app lock and reminders

Private titles and their notes are encrypted with a PIN you choose. The app lock uses your
phone's own fingerprint, face or screen lock; the app never sees your fingerprint or face, only
whether Android confirmed it. Reminder and weekly-review notifications are generated on your
phone and never name your goals.

## Purchases (Plus and tips)

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app
only learns from Google Play whether you own Plus, and remembers that on your phone.

## Optional ads ("Watch an ad to support us")

The app shows an ad only when you tap *Watch an ad to support us* in Settings. Google's ad
service is not started before that. When you do tap it, Google's Mobile Ads SDK loads an ad and,
like any ad service, may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address
and how you interact with the ad, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention; in the
EEA and UK you're asked for consent first. No goal data is ever shared with it. Ads about
alcohol, gambling and dating are blocked. See
[how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Bug reports and feature requests

If you send a report from Settings → Help & feedback, the app opens your own email app or web
browser with the message filled in, and you choose whether to send it. If you include device
details, they are the app version, Android version and phone model. Reports never include
your goals. We use reports only to fix problems and improve the app, and reply from
admin.toolcrate@gmail.com. Messages sent by email or GitHub are also subject to those services'
policies.

If the app crashes, the technical details (the error and where in the app's code it happened,
never your goals or notes) are saved on your phone. Next time you open Stagira it offers to send
them as a bug report, which you see in full and send yourself, or you can dismiss it. Nothing is
sent automatically.

## Test versions from GitHub

Test versions downloaded from the project's GitHub page (not from Google Play) check GitHub's
public release list about twice a day, to tell you when a newer test version is out. That request
carries nothing about you or your ledger; GitHub sees only that a request was made. The version
from Google Play doesn't do this: Play updates it.

## Sharing

We don't sell your data, and we never receive your goals. The only data that reaches a third
party through the app is described above: your own Google Drive if you sync, Google Play when
you buy something, and Google's ad service if you choose to watch an ad.

## Deleting your data

- Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored on your phone.
- To delete the synced copy: Settings → Sync → Disconnect with "Also delete the synced copy"
  ticked, or in Google Drive on the web: Settings → Manage apps → Stagira → Delete hidden app
  data.

## Children

Stagira is intended for adults and isn't directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new
